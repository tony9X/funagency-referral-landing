Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data FunAgency collects through this site and the referral program, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ on Personal Data.

We do not ask for, and do not want, your passwords, advertising account credentials, or payment card details.

2. Browser storage

This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. It stores two values in your browser's local storage:

  • funagency_lang — the language you picked.
  • funagency_cookies_ok — that you dismissed the consent banner.

Both stay on your device, are readable only by this site, and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings. No analytics or advertising scripts are installed on this site at the date above. If we add any, we will ask for consent before they run and update this policy first.

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract — running your participation and paying your commission.
  • Legitimate interests — preventing referral fraud and abuse.
  • Consent — any future analytics, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Legal obligation — keeping financial records where the law requires it.

4. Who else sees it

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with parties we need to operate the Program: Telegram (which carries our bot, under its own policy), our hosting provider, and the blockchain network used for payouts. Note that a USDT transfer is recorded on a public ledger and cannot be erased by us or by anyone else.

5. How long we keep it

  • Participant and referral records: while you take part, then up to 3 years.
  • Payout records: as long as accounting and tax law requires.
  • Enquiries that do not become participation: up to 12 months.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or send it to another provider. Under GDPR you may also complain to your national data protection authority. Under 152-FZ you may withdraw consent and ask that processing stop. Write to hello@funref.com — we reply within 30 days.

Deleting your data ends your participation, and any commission not yet past the holding period is forfeited. Payouts already written to the blockchain cannot be removed.

7. Transfers and security

Our infrastructure may process data outside your country, including outside the EEA and the Russian Federation. Access is limited to staff who need it, and traffic to this site is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your rights we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

8. Children

The Program is not for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Contact

Email hello@funref.com or message @nil_ad01 on Telegram.
